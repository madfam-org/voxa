## Why Mulberry

Voxa is a commercial product. ARASAAC pictograms (used elsewhere in Voxa) are
licensed **CC BY-NC-SA** (non-commercial only). Mulberry Symbols are licensed
**CC BY-SA 4.0**, which permits commercial use with attribution and share-alike.
Mulberry therefore serves as the commercially-clean default imagery source.
